Core Values

Ambition

To me, this means pushing boundaries and challenging the current ideas and perspectives we hold. It’s about setting ambitious goals and striving to surpass them. Throughout my leadership journey, I’ve consistently pushed myself to grow, adapting and rising to new challenges. This mindset has been crucial in opening doors to opportunities and has led me to where I am today.

Resilience

For me, this is about the Cambodian community. It’s about asking, how much do you truly want something, and how far are you willing to go to overcome obstacles? It’s about strengthening a community that was devastated by genocide and empowering each other to rise above adversity. This is deeply important to me because this concept represents both my community and my identity.

Passion

To me, this is what fuels my motivation to get out of bed every morning. Passion isn’t just a single thing; it’s the driving force that gives me endless desire and enthusiasm. This is important because when I’m passionate about something, I’m willing to go to great lengths to achieve my goals.
